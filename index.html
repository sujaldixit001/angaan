Angaan

A relationship and gifting app for people who live far from the people they love.

Distance doesn’t make you care less. It makes the day easier to miss. Angaan gives every person in your life a fruit tree, and the tree grows only when a gift you sent through Angaan is confirmed delivered. Not when you think about it, not when you order it. When it reaches their hands.

Along the way it keeps the small things you tell it, the passing details you would otherwise forget, and uses them to suggest what to send next. Thirty gifts and the tree is in full bloom: a keepsake is unlocked and a new tree can be planted.

Phone prototype

The real prototype, running on this page. Tap, scroll and move through it exactly as you would in the app: open a person, read their gift history, plan something with Angaan, order it, and watch the tree grow when the courier confirms delivery. Every phone in this section is live, here on the page. The circular arrow under a screen sends it back to its first frame.

Where to start. Ananya lives in London. Her mother’s birthday is five days away and it sits at the top of the garden. Open Mum: who she is, what Angaan remembers, every gift ever sent, and her tree at Level 29 of 30.

The one rule. Only a gift ordered through Angaan grows a tree, and only when delivery is confirmed. Go to Orders and watch a live delivery land: the tree gains a level on its own.

Level 30. Mum is one gift away from full bloom. Send it and the keepsake unlocks, ready to share, with a new tree to plant.

Memory. Every note can be read, edited or forgotten, and forgetting can be undone. Profile → Review everything Angaan remembers.

The dashed sun button is a demo control: it previews the four times of day. In production the look follows the device clock.
